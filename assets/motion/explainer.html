The question
Does R&D raise productivity?
Pool all EU countries together and the link looks weak:
the GERD paradox. The thesis asks whether it depends on
which kind of economy spends the money.

3 · Which way causality runs
Countries spend more on R&D after they grow richer
A panel Granger test finds that productivity predicts later R&D, but not the other way round. Naive regressions mistake this feedback for an R&D effect.
